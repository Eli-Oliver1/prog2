Viewport and viewing window

Frustum inputs are in view coordinates at the default near-plane distance (0.5), not world coordinates.

Controls

  • Camera translate: a/d left/right, w/s forward/back, q/e up/down.
  • Camera rotate: A/D yaw left/right, W/S pitch forward/back.
  • Select model: ←/→ previous/next triangle set; Space deselect.
  • Selected model translate: k/; left/right, o/l forward/back, i/p up/down.
  • Selected model rotate: K/: yaw, O/L pitch, I/P roll.
  • Press B to toggle lighting; press ! to show the custom scene (press again to return).
